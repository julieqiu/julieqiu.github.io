    Raw Pad Thai

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 1

    Ingredients

    • 1 zucchini, spiralized
    • 1 carrot, peeled and cut julienne
    • 1/4 cup green cabbage, thinly sliced
    • 1/4 cup red cabbage, thinly sliced
    • 1/2 cup sugar snap peas
    • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
    • 1/4 cup bean sprouts
    • 2 tablespoons minced cilantro

    Dressing

    • 1 1/2 tablespoons raw almond butter
    • 3 tablespoons lime juice
    • 1 tablespoon tamari
    • 1 teaspoon maple syrup or coconut nectar
    • 1/2 teaspoon minced ginger
    • 1 garlic clove, minced
    • 1/4 cup raw cashews or almonds, crushed

    Instructions

    1. In a bowl, combine the vegetables.

    2. In another small bowl, whisk together the dressing ingredients, adding enough water to reach a dressing consistency.

    3. Toss the dressing with the vegetables and transfer to a plate. Sprinkle with the crushed nuts and serve.
