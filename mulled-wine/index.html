    Mulled Wine

    Source: NYT Cooking — Mulled Wine, by Rosie Schaap

    Choose a red that isn’t bone-dry — a little fruitiness is fine here.

    Yield: 6 to 8 servings Time: 45 minutes

    Ingredients

    • 2 large oranges
    • 4 1/4 cups (1 liter) red wine
    • 1 1/4 cups (10 ounces) brandy
    • 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
    • 2 cinnamon sticks
    • 6 whole cloves
    • 3 cardamom pods, slightly crushed

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: peel and juice one orange, slice the other into rounds for garnish. Combine peel, juice, and everything else in a nonreactive pan, stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves, bring to a boil, then simmer gently 30 minutes. Strain and serve.
