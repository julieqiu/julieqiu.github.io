    Mediterranean Bean Salad

    Source: Cookie and Kate — Mediterranean Bean Salad

    Crisp and satisfying, and it holds up well — leftovers keep about 4 days. Adapted by the original author from Ethnic Cuisine by Elisabeth Rozin.

    Prep Time: 20 minutes Yield: about six 1-cup servings

    Ingredients

    • 2 cans red kidney beans (15 ounces each), rinsed and drained, or 3 cups cooked kidney beans
    • 1 can chickpeas (15 ounces), rinsed and drained, or 1 1/2 cups cooked chickpeas
    • 1 small red onion, diced (about 1 cup)
    • 2 stalks celery, sliced in half or thirds lengthwise and chopped (about 3/4 cup)
    • 1 medium cucumber, peeled, seeded and diced
    • 3/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
    • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill or mint
    • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
    • 1/4 cup lemon juice (about 1 1/2 lemons)
    • 3 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
    • 3/4 teaspoon fine salt
    • Small pinch red pepper flakes

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: combine beans, chickpeas and chopped vegetables in a serving bowl. Whisk the dressing separately, pour over and stir. Best after a couple of hours marinating in the fridge. If leftovers taste flat, wake them up with a little salt or lemon.
