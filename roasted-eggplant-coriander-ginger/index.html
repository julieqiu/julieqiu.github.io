    Roasted Eggplant with Coriander and Ginger

    Source: Sur La Table cooking class, “Secrets of Indian Cooking” with Chef Scott (November 2018)

    Adjust the level of heat to suit your preference by using more or less jalapeño. Seeding and removing the membrane of the jalapeño pepper decreases the heat level.

    Servings: 6

    Ingredients

    • 1 large eggplant, halved lengthwise
    • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
    • 2 tablespoons coconut oil or ghee
    • 1 cup finely chopped yellow onion
    • 3 tablespoons minced ginger
    • 1 teaspoon toasted and ground cumin seeds
    • 1 teaspoon toasted and ground coriander seeds
    • 4 garlic cloves, minced
    • 1 fresh jalapeño chile, seeded and chopped
    • 1 (14-ounce) can of diced tomatoes, drained
    • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
    • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
    • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro

    Instructions

    1. Preheat the oven to broil with the rack positioned in the center. Place the eggplant, cut side down, on a greased baking sheet. Pierce the eggplant all over with a paring knife or a fork. Transfer eggplant to the oven and broil until the skin is blackened and the eggplant collapses, 15 to 20 minutes; remove from the oven and set aside to cool. When the eggplant is cool enough to handle, use a spoon to scoop pulp from eggplant halves into a medium bowl; discard skins, mash the pulp and mix in lime juice.

    2. To a medium skillet set over medium-high heat, add oil. When oil melts, add onion and cook, stirring often, until the onion is golden brown, about 6 minutes. Add the ginger, cumin, coriander, garlic, and chile, sauté until aromatic, about 1 more minute. Stir in tomatoes and turmeric and cook until heated through, about 5 minutes. Stir in the eggplant purée and cook, stirring, 3 to 5 minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Off heat, stir in cilantro and serve with naan or over rice.
