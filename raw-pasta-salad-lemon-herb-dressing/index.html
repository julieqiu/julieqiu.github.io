    Raw Pasta Salad with Creamy Lemon and Herb Dressing

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Ingredients

    Salad

    • 1 zucchini, spiralized
    • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, broccoli, green beans, cucumber, radishes, tomatoes, pea pods, bell peppers — whatever you have)
    • Optional: 1/2 cup protein (edamame, diced tofu, garbanzo/white/navy/cannellini beans, quinoa)

    Dressing

    • 1/4 cup mayo (regular, soy, Vegenaise)
    • 2–3 tablespoons honey (or maple syrup, agave, brown rice syrup to keep it vegan)
    • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
    • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
    • 1 teaspoon basil, fresh or dried
    • 1/2 teaspoon dill
    • 1/2 teaspoon fresh-ground pepper, or to taste
    • Pinch of salt, optional

    Instructions

    1. Spiralize the zucchini.

    2. Add the diced vegetables and protein.

    3. Stir the dressing ingredients to combine, then coat and toss the salad.

    Notes

    Dressing it 15–20 minutes before eating softens the noodles and gives them a more cooked texture. Or microwave the whole plate for 30 seconds if you want it hot.
