    Tangled Thai Salad

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 1

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup chopped napa cabbage
    • 1/3 cup sliced jicama, cut into small sticks
    • 2/3 cup shredded or spiralized carrot
    • 2/3 cup shredded or spiralized yellow beet
    • 4 tablespoons peanut lime dressing (below)
    • 3 slices cucumber, halved
    • 2 teaspoons chopped raw peanuts
    • 2 tablespoons fresh salad topper (below)
    • 1 lime wedge
    • 2 to 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro

    Peanut lime dressing

    Makes 1 cup. Keeps up to a week in the fridge — also good on any salad, or poured over cold noodles.

    • 3 cloves garlic, minced (about 1 tablespoon)
    • 2 tablespoons tightly packed cilantro
    • 2 tablespoons natural peanut butter
    • 2 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons lime juice
    • 1 tablespoon plus 1/2 teaspoon tamari
    • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
    • 1 tablespoon peeled and chopped ginger
    • 2 teaspoons coconut milk
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons organic sugar
    • 3/4 teaspoon sesame oil
    • 3/4 teaspoon sambal oelek
    • 1/2 cup sunflower oil

    Fresh salad topper

    Makes about 1 3/4 cups. Keeps for months in an airtight container.

    • 1 cup puffed quinoa (puffed millet works too)
    • 1/4 cup goji berries
    • 1/4 cup currants
    • 2 tablespoons sliced almonds
    • 2 tablespoons chopped hazelnuts
    • 2 tablespoons chopped pistachios
    • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

    Instructions

    1. For the dressing, purée all ingredients except the sunflower oil in a blender. With the blender running, add the sunflower oil in a thin stream until the dressing is emulsified.

    2. For the topper, put all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.

    3. Put the napa cabbage in a large, shallow serving bowl or plate. Top with jicama.

    4. Pile the carrot and beet on top and drizzle with dressing.

    5. Garnish with the cucumber, peanuts, salad topper, lime, and cilantro.

    Notes

    For a fruit salad, yogurt, or oatmeal topper, leave the salt out of the mix and replace it with sugar.
