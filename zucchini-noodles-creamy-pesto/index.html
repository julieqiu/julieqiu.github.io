    Zucchini Noodles with Creamy Pesto Sauce

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Ingredients

    • 2–3 medium zucchini, spiralized
    • 1/2 cup fresh basil
    • 1/4 cup raw pine nuts
    • 1/4 cup olive oil
    • 2 cloves raw garlic
    • 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast or grated pumpkin seeds

    Instructions

    1. Blend all ingredients except the zucchini in a food processor until smooth.

    2. Scoop the zucchini noodles into bowls and top with the pesto sauce.

    Notes

    Add vegetables for flavor, color and texture — olives, sun-dried tomatoes, mushrooms, artichoke, or colorful peppers.
