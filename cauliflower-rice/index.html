    Cauliflower Rice

    Source: NYT Cooking — Cauliflower Rice, by Ali Slagle

    Roasting rather than steaming is what makes this worth doing — the raw edges caramelize. Keeps about a week refrigerated, a month frozen.

    Yield: 10 to 12 cups raw; 4 to 5 cups cooked Time: 45 minutes

    Ingredients

    • 2 medium cauliflowers (about 4 pounds total), cored and cut into florets
    • 6 tablespoons olive oil
    • 2 large garlic cloves, finely chopped (about 1 tablespoon), optional
    • Kosher salt and black pepper

    Ginger-garlic sauce

    • 1 1/2 tablespoons canola or other neutral oil
    • 1 (2-inch) piece ginger, peeled and cut into thin matchsticks
    • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons sesame oil
    • 1/2 teaspoon red-pepper flakes
    • Kosher salt

    Tahini-Parmesan sauce

    • 3 tablespoons tahini
    • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
    • 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: pulse florets in a food processor, half a batch at a time, until they resemble rice. Toss with oil and garlic, spread across two sheet pans, and roast at 425°F for 15–20 minutes until golden at the edges. Both sauces are made separately on the stovetop and poured over before serving.
