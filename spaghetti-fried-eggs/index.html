    Spaghetti with Fried Eggs

    Source: NYT Cooking — Spaghetti With Fried Eggs, by Mark Bittman

    For when you have little time and even less food in the house. All you need is spaghetti, four eggs, olive oil, and garlic.

    Yield: 2 or 3 servings Time: 20 minutes

    Ingredients

    • Salt
    • 1/2 pound thin spaghetti
    • 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil or lard
    • 2 large cloves garlic, lightly smashed and peeled
    • 4 eggs
    • Freshly ground black pepper
    • Freshly grated Parmesan or pecorino cheese, optional

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: infuse 4 tablespoons of the oil with garlic over medium-low heat, pressing it in occasionally; remove the garlic and add the rest of the oil. Fry the eggs gently until the whites just set and the yolks stay runny. Drain the pasta and toss with the eggs and oil, breaking up the whites — they finish cooking in the heat of the pasta.
