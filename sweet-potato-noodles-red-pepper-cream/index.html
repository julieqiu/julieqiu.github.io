    Sweet Potato Noodles with Roasted Red Pepper Cream Sauce

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 2

    Ingredients

    • 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled (save 1/2 potato for the sauce)
    • 1 tablespoon butter or coconut oil
    • 1 cup chopped asparagus
    • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
    • Sea salt, to taste

    Roasted red pepper cream sauce

    Makes about 2 cups.

    • 1 cup steamed sweet potato chunks
    • 1 cup nut milk or non-dairy milk of choice
    • 1 teaspoon dried basil
    • 2 cloves raw garlic
    • 1 roasted red pepper
    • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

    Instructions

    1. Spiralize 1 1/2 of the sweet potatoes into noodles. Steam the remaining 1/2 potato — about one cup of chunks — for the sauce.

    2. For the sauce, blend all sauce ingredients in a high-powered blender until smooth and creamy. Adjust seasonings to taste.

    3. Sauté the asparagus and mushrooms in a bit of butter until tender. A splash of water keeps things moving without adding more butter.

    4. Add the sweet potato noodles and cook just long enough for them to soften slightly without going mushy.

    5. Add the cream sauce immediately and toss to coat. A lid on the pan for about 2 minutes gently steams the noodles and heats everything through.

    6. Adjust seasonings and serve immediately.

    Notes

    Make the noodles while the red peppers steam for the sauce.

    If the sweet potato is freshly steamed, be careful blending — steam pressure can pop the lid off. Cold nut milk helps cool it down.
