    Stir-Fried Sesame Shrimp and Spinach

    Source: NYT Cooking — Stir-Fried Sesame Shrimp and Spinach, by Martha Rose Shulman

    The classic Chinese way to clean shrimp — and ensure succulent flavor and crisp texture — is to rub them with salt and rinse. Bunch spinach works better here than bagged.

    Yield: 3 to 4 servings Time: About 15 minutes

    Ingredients

    • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
    • Salt to taste
    • 1/8 teaspoon sugar
    • 2 tablespoons canola oil or light sesame oil
    • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
    • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
    • 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon crumbled dried red chili
    • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
    • 1 generous bunch spinach (about 1 pound), stems trimmed at the end, rinsed in 2 changes water
    • 2 teaspoons dark sesame oil

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: scrub the shrimp with salt, rinse twice, pat very dry. Get the wok ripping hot, stir-fry garlic, ginger and chili for ten seconds, then add the shrimp in one layer and leave undisturbed a minute. Add sesame seeds and spinach, stir-fry until just wilted and the shrimp are pink, then finish off-heat with dark sesame oil.
