    Healthy Pasta Salad

    Source: The Big Man’s World

    Prep Time: 10 minutes Total Time: 10 minutes Servings: 8

    Ingredients

    Salad

    • 8 1/2 oz fusilli pasta (or any pasta)
    • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
    • 1 large red onion, sliced
    • 1 medium cucumber, sliced
    • 1/4 cup black olives, pitted
    • 1 large red bell pepper, diced
    • 1/2 cup feta cheese
    • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, minced

    Dressing

    • 1 clove garlic, minced
    • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
    • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/8 tsp black pepper
    • 1 small lemon, juiced
    • 2 Tbsp olive oil
    • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
    • 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard

    Instructions

    1. Cook pasta according to package directions until al dente (approximately 8 minutes). Drain in a colander.

    2. Prepare dressing by combining minced garlic, thyme, oregano, salt, lemon juice, olive oil, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, and black pepper in a small bowl.

    3. Mix cooked pasta with tomatoes, onion, cucumber, olives, and bell pepper in a large bowl. Pour dressing over and combine.

    4. Crumble feta cheese over salad and garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

    Nutrition

    Per serving: 211 calories, 32g carbs, 6g protein, 7g fat
