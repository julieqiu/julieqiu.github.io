    Utica Greens

    Source: NYT Cooking — Utica Greens, by Jim Shahin

    Popularized by Joe Morelle at the Chesterfield Restaurant in Utica, N.Y. in the late 1980s, where it’s on the menu as greens Morelle. This version is fairly spicy — use fewer cherry peppers if you prefer it milder. Usually served as an appetizer, but it makes a good main course with crusty bread and red wine.

    Yield: 4 servings Time: 35 minutes

    Ingredients

    Oreganato

    • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
    • 1 cup bread crumbs
    • 1/2 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated

    Greens

    • 1 head escarole, about 1 1/4 pounds, bottom removed, leaves separated and washed thoroughly to remove grit
    • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
    • 4 slices prosciutto (about 2 ounces), sliced thin and cut into roughly 1-inch squares
    • 4 to 6 hot cherry peppers (pickled will do if you can’t find fresh), tops and seeds removed, broken by hand or chopped into 4 or 5 chunks
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
    • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: mix the oreganato to the texture of moist beach sand and set aside. Blanch the escarole 1–2 minutes, shock in ice water, drain and chop. Sauté prosciutto, cherry peppers and garlic until the prosciutto crisps, add the greens, then top with oreganato and run under the broiler.

    You’ll have leftover oreganato — use it on another greens dish, or on baked chicken or shrimp.
