    Roasted Carrot Pasta with Beets

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 1–2

    Ingredients

    • 2 beets
    • 3 large carrots, spiralized into spaghetti strands
    • 2 small shallots, minced
    • 1 tablespoon plain goat cheese
    • 2–3 tablespoons fresh thyme
    • 1 1/2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
    • Olive oil
    • Salt and pepper

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F.

    2. Wash and clean the outside of the beets. Place on a small sheet of foil, drizzle with a small amount of olive oil and salt, and wrap in foil. Place on a sheet pan and roast for 1 hour.

    3. Over a clean bowl, spiralize the carrots. Add fresh thyme, olive oil, salt, pepper, and minced shallots. Mix together.

    4. Spread the carrot mixture on a baking sheet and roast 10–15 minutes.

    5. The beets are done when a fork goes through the foil easily. Let cool, then peel back the outer layer and slice each beet into about 5 slices.

    6. Return the roasted carrots to the bowl. Stir in the beets, goat cheese, and balsamic vinegar.
