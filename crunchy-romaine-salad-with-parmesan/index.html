    Crunchy Romaine Salad with Parmesan

    Source: The Galley Gourmet

    Total Time: 5 minutes Servings: 4

    Ingredients

    Vinaigrette

    • 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
    • 6 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
    • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
    • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
    • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional)

    Salad

    • 2 hearts of romaine lettuce, trimmed
    • 3/4 to 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
    • 2 small shallots, peeled and thinly sliced

    Instructions

    1. Tear, chop, or thinly slice the romaine lettuce. Wash and spin or pat dry thoroughly.

    2. In a jar or small bowl, combine the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using).

    3. Place the lettuce, Parmesan, and shallots in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over and toss to combine. Serve immediately.

    Notes

    • Keep components separate until serving to maintain crispness.
