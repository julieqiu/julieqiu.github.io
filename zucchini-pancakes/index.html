    Zucchini Pancakes

    Source: NYT Cooking — Zucchini Pancakes, by Elaine Louie

    Mücver (pronounced moosh-vair) are delicate, crisp zucchini pancakes popular in Turkey. The trick to keeping them crisp rather than soggy is squeezing all the water out of the zucchini before mixing — a little brute force is required.

    Yield: 12 pancakes Time: 30 minutes

    Ingredients

    Pancakes

    • 3 medium zucchini, shredded
    • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
    • 3 large eggs, beaten
    • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
    • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
    • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
    • 3 scallions, finely chopped
    • 1/3 cup finely chopped dill
    • 1 teaspoon baking powder
    • 4 to 6 tablespoons vegetable oil, more as needed

    Yogurt sauce

    • 2/3 cup plain yogurt
    • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt

    Method

    Full instructions at the source link.

    In short: salt the shredded zucchini and drain 5 minutes, then wring it out hard in a kitchen towel — twice. Volume should halve. Mix with eggs, then flour, salt, oil, feta, scallions, dill and pepper, adding baking powder last. Fry heaping tablespoons in hot oil about 5–6 minutes total, flipping a few times until quite crisp, and hold in a 250°F oven. Serve with the garlic yogurt.
