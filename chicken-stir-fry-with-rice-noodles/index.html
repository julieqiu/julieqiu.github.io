    Chicken Stir Fry with Rice Noodles

    Source: Natasha’s Kitchen

    Prep Time: 20 minutes Cook Time: 10 minutes Total Time: 30 minutes Servings: 4

    Ingredients

    Noodles

    • 12 oz rice noodles
    • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

    Stir Fry

    • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
    • 1 lb skinless boneless chicken breasts, sliced into bite-sized strips
    • Salt and black pepper, to taste
    • 1 red bell pepper, sliced (or 1/2 red and 1/2 green)
    • 1 cup broccoli, chopped
    • 4 oz shiitake or portobello mushrooms, sliced
    • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, peeled and grated

    Sauce

    • 1/2 cup chicken broth
    • 2 tablespoons soy sauce (not low-sodium)
    • 2 tablespoons ketchup
    • 1 tsp cornstarch
    • Sesame oil, for drizzling

    Instructions

    1. Boil salted water, add rice noodles, and cook 2-4 minutes until barely tender. Drain and stir in 1 tablespoon vegetable oil.

    2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a wok over high heat. Season chicken with salt and pepper, stir-fry for 3 minutes until cooked through. Remove and set aside.

    3. Add bell peppers, broccoli, and mushrooms to the wok. Sauté for 1 minute. Add ginger and stir-fry 2 more minutes.

    4. In a small bowl, combine chicken broth, soy sauce, ketchup, and cornstarch.

    5. Add chicken, noodles, and sauce mixture to the wok. Stir-fry for 3 minutes until heated through.

    6. Drizzle with sesame oil before serving. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

    Notes

    • Straight cut rice noodles can be found in the Asian section of most grocery stores.
    • If using low-sodium soy sauce or broth, season to taste with salt or the dish will be lacking in flavor.
