    Garlic-Zucchini Pasta

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Ingredients

    • 2 large or 3 small-medium zucchini
    • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1–2 tablespoons olive oil
    • 1 pound angel hair or thin spaghetti
    • 1 cup grated or shredded parmesan cheese
    • 2–3 grilled chicken breasts

    Instructions

    1. Cook the chicken breasts on the grill, seasoned with a little salt. Set aside.

    2. Spiralize the zucchini into noodles.

    3. Prepare the angel hair or spaghetti as directed on the box.

    4. While the pasta cooks, sauté the garlic in olive oil for a minute or two. Add the zucchini and sauté 10 to 15 minutes longer.

    5. Add the cooked pasta and parmesan and mix well. Cut the chicken into strips.

    6. Plate the pasta, top with chicken and extra cheese if you like. Serve with salad or bread.
