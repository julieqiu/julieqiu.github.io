    Green Smoothie

    Source: JoyFoodSunshine

    Total Time: 5 minutes Servings: 2

    Ingredients

    • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened almond milk
    • 2 cups spinach (frozen)
    • 1 medium banana (frozen)
    • 1 cup fruit of choice (frozen)

    Instructions

    1. Put ingredients into the container of a Vitamix blender in the order listed.

    2. Start blending on low speed and increase to high.

    3. Blend on high speed for 50-60 seconds until mixture is smooth.

    4. Pour into glasses and enjoy!
