    Beet and Apple Spirals

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 2

    Ingredients

    • 2 medium apples, spiralized
    • 2 beets, spiralized
    • 1 large carrot, spiralized
    • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
    • 1/4 cup sunflower seeds
    • 1/2 cup balsamic honey dressing

    Instructions

    1. Toss all of the ingredients together and serve.
