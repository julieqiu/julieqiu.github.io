    Avocado Green Goddess Dip with Crudité

    Source: Sur La Table cooking class

    The dressing can be made ahead of time and refrigerated for up to 5 days.

    Servings: 4

    Ingredients

    Dressing

    • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
    • 1/3 cup buttermilk
    • 1 avocado, pitted and diced
    • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced green onions
    • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
    • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh basil leaves
    • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
    • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
    • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
    • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

    Crudité

    • 2 celery stalks cut into 3-inch sticks
    • 2 carrots cut into 3-inch sticks
    • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets, blanched and shocked
    • 1 demi baguette cut on the bias into 1/2-inch slices

    Instructions

    1. Combine all of the ingredients for the dressing in the bowl of a blender or food processor and blend or pulse until smooth, 30 to 45 seconds. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Transfer to a serving bowl.

    2. Arrange cut vegetables on a platter with sliced baguette and bowl of dip. Serve at room temperature or chilled.
