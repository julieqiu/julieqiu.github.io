    Zucchini Pasta with Sweet Pepper Marinara

    Source: Spiralizer recipe booklet

    Servings: 2

    Ingredients

    • 2 medium zucchini, spiralized

    Sauce

    • 1 very large red or yellow bell pepper, deseeded
    • 3/4 cup cherry or roma tomatoes
    • 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes
    • 1 tablespoon olive oil
    • 1/4 teaspoon salt
    • Generous sprinkle dried thyme
    • Generous sprinkle dried oregano
    • 1/4 cup basil
    • Stevia to taste

    Instructions

    1. Blend all sauce ingredients in a high speed blender until thick and smooth.

    2. Serve over the spiralized zucchini.

    Notes

    Also good with raw nut meatballs, chunks of avocado, or any other mix-ins.
